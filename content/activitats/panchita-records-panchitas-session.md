#### Panchita’s Session – Panchita Soul Adventurers meets Harny Roots + Convidats + Badalonians Sound

## Roots Rock Reggae Dub Party

**Diumenge 22**de gener **Panchita Records**presenta la primera **Panchita’s Session**, un dub amb Panchita Soul Adventures (reggae/roots band) Harny Roots i Badalonians Sound.

- [Entrada: 5€ anticipada](https://www.notikumi.com/channel/la-panchita-records-s-l/2023/1/22/panchitas-session)

Començarà la session [Badalonians Sound](https://www.instagram.com/badalonians_sound/?next=%2Fblackandwhitevintage108%2F) amb la seva increïble selecció de reggae/roots i seguiran Soul Adventures.

Tindrem la banda de @l[apanchitarecords](https://www.instagram.com/lapanchitarecords/?next=%2Fblackandwhitevintage108%2F) acompanyant a [Harny Roots](https://www.instagram.com/harny_roots/?next=%2Fblackandwhitevintage108%2F) i artistes convidades com [Adala](https://www.instagram.com/adala.blankfosk/?next=%2Fblackandwhitevintage108%2F), [Matah](https://www.instagram.com/matahsound/?next=%2Fblackandwhitevintage108%2F), [Eipriil ](https://www.instagram.com/eipriil/?next=%2Fblackandwhitevintage108%2F)i [Misha1Dem](https://www.instagram.com/misha.1dem/?next=%2Fblackandwhitevintage108%2F)
